MTN Nigeria USSD Toolkit

Keep your MTN line ready for client demos, remote work, and product support. These USSD combinations work on feature phones and smartphones alike, even without data connectivity.

Essential MTN USSD Commands
Action USSD Code Quick Notes
Recharge airtime from scratch card *555*PIN# PIN is the 12-digit number on your MTN recharge card. Confirm balance afterwards with *556#.
Borrow airtime (XtraTime) *606# Pick a borrowing option and repay automatically on your next recharge.
Buy data bundle *312# Follow prompts for daily, weekly, monthly plans, or streaming bundles.
Check data balance *323# or *310# Both codes show remaining data volume and expiry date instantly.
Check airtime balance *310# Displays current airtime and bonus balances.
MTN customer care *300# or call 300 Ideal for resolving service disruptions or SIM swap updates.
Share airtime (Share & Sell) *321*Recipient Number*Amount*PIN# Default PIN is 0000; change it via *321*2# for security.
Link NIN (National Identity Number) *996# Keep your line compliant; follow prompts to submit or update your NIN.

Pro tips

  • Save the USSD codes as contacts (e.g., “MTN Data”) to dial them faster during remote troubleshooting.
